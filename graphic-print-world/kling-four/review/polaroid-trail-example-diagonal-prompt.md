# Polaroid trail — diagonal scatter example

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: visual layout concept for a JuiceBuilder scrollytelling website
Primary request: Create one wide landscape concept image showing a loose trail of Polaroid-style influencer snapshots scattered across a warm handmade-paper background. The snapshots should feel physically thrown down and landing unordered: varied rotations, some overlapping and partially covering others, uneven spacing, a gentle diagonal path that travels from lower-left toward upper-right, with a few frames drifting toward the horizontal edges.
Scene/backdrop: warm cream tactile paper, no table horizon, a single flat paper surface.
Subject: six to eight off-white Polaroid prints with thick cream borders and blank caption strips, each containing a different hand-printed creator scene: pouring citrus and mint, adding herbs to a blender, presenting a green botanical drink, arranging ginger and leaves, filming a recipe on a phone tripod, smiling with a finished ruby hibiscus drink.
Style/medium: bold vintage screen-print / linocut collage; deep cobalt, vermilion, orange, leaf-green and cream inks; imperfect registration, halftone dots, carved hatching, ink scratches, torn paper fibres and tactile print texture. All people, hands, drinks and ingredients are graphic illustrations, never photographs.
Composition/framing: wide 16:9; clearly show the full scattered arrangement and overlaps; preserve generous cream negative space between clusters; make it feel like a trail that could later move horizontally with scroll.
Lighting/mood: flat printed daylight, editorial and playful.
Constraints: no continuous filmstrip rail, no sprocket holes, no rigid grid, no captions, logos, watermarks, medical symbols or extra lettering.
