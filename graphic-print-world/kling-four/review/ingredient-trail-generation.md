# Ingredient trail assets

Saved prototype: the current gap uses the inspiration filmstrip instead. The original trail markup is retained in the `#saved-ingredient-trail` template in `index.html`, with its assets, CSS and JavaScript preserved for a future section. It is not currently displayed.

The transparent cutouts float within the existing paper gap. The centre stays open; no spacer or background panel is inserted. The three scroll depths and rotations can be adjusted with `data-drift-x`, `data-drift-y`, and `data-drift-turn` in `index.html`. Static placement and responsive sizes are in `scroll.css`. `ingredient-trail.js` is separate from video scrubbing and text animations. Mobile shows orange and mint; reduced-motion preferences retain static ingredients.

### trail-orange

Reference: `panels/discover-board-landscape-v2.png`.

Subject: ONE single round orange slice, slightly oblique, with vivid orange juicy radial segments, cream pith and a fine textured orange rind. The visual should resemble a carefully cut-out printed illustration taken from the reference collage. No leaves, no other slices, no text. Asset type: an individual transparent PNG cutout for a premium JuiceBuilder website parallax ingredient trail. Image 1 is a visual style reference only. Match its textured food illustration, richly coloured vintage print, subtle halftone, imperfect ink registration, tactile cut-paper silhouette and detailed natural food surfaces. The only opaque pixels should belong to the ingredient itself. Real transparent alpha background, no beige or white square, no checkerboard drawn into the image, no paper card, no coloured paper backdrop, no typography, no labels, no watermark, no tabletop or cast shadow. One complete isolated ingredient motif, centered, all edges intact and comfortably inside the canvas, filling approximately 85% of a square canvas. It will be viewed as a small floating element, so make its silhouette legible and clean.
