# Polaroid trail — diverse creators, normal instant-film shape

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: visual layout concept for a JuiceBuilder scrollytelling website
Primary request: Create a dense scattered trail of ordinary instant-film Polaroid photographs on warm handmade paper. Use the familiar portrait Polaroid shape: thick off-white border on all four sides and a noticeably deeper blank white caption strip along the bottom. Arrange seven separate Polaroids as if casually thrown down, with several overlapping, different rotations and a loose winding trail from left to right.
People and actions: show a visibly diverse group of adult drink creators with varied ethnic backgrounds, skin tones, facial features, hair textures and hairstyles. Give each picture a genuinely different action: filming a recipe on a phone tripod; washing and arranging herbs; slicing citrus and ginger; pouring a drink; adding botanicals to a blender; tasting a green herbal drink; and proudly presenting a finished hibiscus bottle.
Style/medium: bold JuiceBuilder hand-printed linocut / vintage screen-print collage; cobalt, vermilion, orange, leaf-green and cream inks; imperfect registration, halftone dots, carved hatching, ink scratches, tactile paper grain and subtle paper shadows.
Composition/framing: wide landscape 16:9, cream negative space around the trail, physically believable overlaps, varied rotations around -18 to +18 degrees, every Polaroid legible, no rigid row.
Constraints: normal Polaroid proportions and borders must be unmistakable; no continuous rail, sprocket holes, logos, watermarks, medical symbols, UI or lettering.
